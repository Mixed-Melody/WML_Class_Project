Why Choose Dragon's Den College?

Dragon's Den College gives students the opportunity to learn practical skills in a supportive and welcoming environment. Our programs are designed to prepare students for their future while giving them the chance to discover what they are capable of.

Students at Dragon's Den College have access to experienced instructors, hands-on learning, and resources that can help them reach their goals. Your future begins with the first step, and we are ready to help you take it.

Steps to Apply

  1. Complete the online application form.
  2. Submit all required documents.
  3. Review your application for accuracy.
  4. Submit your application before the deadline.
  5. Wait for your admissions decision.

Required Documents

  • Official transcripts from previous institutions
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Personal statement or essay
  • Standardized test scores, if applicable

Important Dates

Application deadline: March 31, 2027.
Fall semester begins: August 23, 2027
