Advanced Fire Control

Mastering fire is one of the most important skills a dragon can learn. The Advanced Fire Control program teaches students how to control the strength, range, and accuracy of their flames in a variety of situations. Students practice everything from lighting a campfire without destroying the campsite to safely using fire for defense. Courses also cover fire safety, environmental awareness, and what to do when a sneeze goes terribly wrong. Graduates leave the program with greater confidence in their abilities and a much lower chance of accidentally setting the village on fire.

Program Duration: 2 years
Tuition: $15,000 per year
Prerequisites: Basic Fire Control

Treasure Management

A successful hoard requires more than simply piling gold in the nearest cave. The Treasure Management program teaches dragons how to collect, organize, protect, and manage their valuables. Students learn how to evaluate treasure, maintain accurate inventories, identify counterfeit coins, and design secure storage systems. Advanced courses explore investment strategies and the difficult question of how many jeweled goblets one dragon actually needs. Whether students maintain a modest collection or dream of managing a legendary hoard, this program provides the skills needed to keep their treasure safe and organized.

Program Duration: 2 years
Tuition: $15,000 per year
Prerequisites: None

Knight Avoidance and Conflict Resolution

Not every encounter with an armored adventurer needs to end in fire and property damage. The Knight Avoidance and Conflict Resolution program teaches dragons how to recognize dangerous situations and resolve conflicts before someone loses a tower. Students study knight behavior, negotiation techniques, escape strategies, and the warning signs of an approaching adventuring party. Practical exercises allow students to practice handling common situations without immediately resorting to fire breathing. Graduates are prepared to protect themselves, their communities, and their hoards while avoiding unnecessary confrontations with the kingdom's more enthusiastic heroes.

Program Duration: 3 years
Tuition: $10,000 per year
Prerequisites: None

Lair Design and Defense

Every dragon deserves a comfortable and secure place to call home. The Lair Design and Defense program combines practical construction skills with defensive planning to help students create lairs that are both comfortable and difficult to invade. Students learn cave selection, entrance design, treasure chamber construction, trap placement, ventilation, and defensive strategy. Special attention is given to designing spaces that accommodate wings, tails, and growing treasure collections. By graduation, students will be prepared to design a lair capable of surviving harsh weather, curious villagers, and the occasional poorly prepared adventuring party.

Program Duration: 1 year
Tuition: $10,000 per year
Prerequisites: None

Responsible Pillaging and Resource Acquisition

Pillaging has a long history in dragon culture, but modern dragons must understand that taking everything that isn't nailed down can create unnecessary problems with neighboring communities. The Responsible Pillaging and Resource Acquisition program combines traditional dragon practices with modern approaches to resource gathering. Students study planning, navigation, treasure identification, local customs, and techniques for minimizing unnecessary property damage. The program also explores alternatives to traditional pillaging, including trade, employment, treasure hunting, and negotiated tribute. Graduates leave prepared to acquire the resources they need without accidentally starting a war every other Tuesday.

Program Duration: 1 year
Tuition: $5,000 per year
Prerequisites: None
